The game

Prepare the party.
Risk the expedition.

Mercenary Company combines tactical party preparation, autonomous dungeon expeditions and extraction-driven risk. What your mercenaries bring home shapes the next expedition.

A party of four

Recruit mercenaries and prepare their equipment and formation.

Hostile dungeons

Watch your party explore and fight according to the policy you set.

Extraction risk

Equipment carried into the dungeon is at risk until the party extracts.

Company management

Manage your roster, equipment and storage between expeditions.

01 / Dungeon expeditions

Watch the expedition unfold.

Monitor party health, carried equipment and the dungeon map as your mercenaries explore and fight autonomously.

Survival and extraction determine what returns to the company.

02 / Party preparation

Plan before you depart.

Choose an expedition policy, tune tactical priorities, arrange the party’s formation and prepare supplies.

Your decisions begin before the party enters the dungeon.

03 / Company management

Build the company behind the party.

Recruit mercenaries, compare their skills and attributes, and manage equipment and storage between expeditions.

Prepare the people and resources you will send into the next dungeon.
